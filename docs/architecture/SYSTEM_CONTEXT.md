# System Context

Paejae Pick 2 is a smart campus app prototype for Paejae University students.

## Users

- Students
- Clubs
- Departments
- Campus service operators
- Reviewers

## Service Areas

- Campus map
- Student notices
- Club information
- QR missions
- Cafeteria concept
- Authentication and privacy planning

## Current Boundary

Paejae Pick 2 is not an official university service unless reviewed and approved by the appropriate university departments.
